How do I install Ceedoku with my browser?

Note

Firefox does not support installing Ceedoku via your web browser
Do not try to use firefox to install Ceedoku as it will not work.

Step 1:

Open Ceedoku in your browser.

Step 2:

Click the button with the 3 dots (or a similar menu style button).

Step 3:

Click the option that says: 'Install/Add Shortcut' (If it is not visible, scroll down).

Step 4:

Click 'Install' and wait for the app to have installed, then proceed.

Step 5:

Locate the app on your homescreen and open it.

And, you're done! Have fun playing Ceedoku! =D
