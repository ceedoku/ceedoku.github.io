Sudoku, without the nonsense.

Ceedoku is a free, lightweight, open-source Sudoku game designed to give you a simple and powerful Sudoku experience.

What is Ceedoku?

Ceedoku is a free, lightweight, and open-source Sudoku game made by Ceebug. It is designed to fit your needs while using as few resources as possible, allowing Ceedoku to run on a wide range of devices.

With multiple difficulty levels, hints, saving, dark mode, animations, haptics, sound effects, and more, Ceedoku gives you a simple yet powerful Sudoku experience.

Why Ceedoku?

No ads Play Sudoku without advertisements getting in the way.
Works offline Install Ceedoku and play even without an internet connection.
8 difficulties From Easy all the way up to Godlike.
Game saving Save your game and continue it later.
Dark & light mode Choose the appearance you prefer.
Hints Get help when you need it, with hint cooldowns.

Get Ceedoku

Play Ceedoku directly in your browser, install it as an app, or download the executable version for your OS.
